# Item Identification (SKU Management) and Core Store Keeping

## Feature 1: Stock Location Management

### Short Description

Stock Location Management is a feature that allows an inventory management system to record and track the physical location of items within a store or warehouse. It helps the storekeeper know exactly where a particular item is kept.

### Purpose

The purpose of Stock Location Management is to make it easier to locate items quickly and maintain an organized store. It reduces the time spent searching for products and helps prevent items from being misplaced.

### Main Functions/Activities

- Record the physical location of each item in the store.
- Assign items to specific shelves, racks, aisles, rooms, or storage areas.
- Search for an item's location using its SKU or product name.
- Update an item's location when it is moved.
- Track the movement of items between different storage locations.
- Organize items according to their assigned storage locations.
- Help storekeepers quickly locate items when they are needed.
- Maintain accurate records of where inventory is stored.


## Feature 2: Stock Adjustment

### Short Description

Stock Adjustment is a feature that allows a storekeeper to correct the recorded quantity of an item when it differs from the actual quantity available in the store. Adjustments may be required because of damaged goods, lost items, expired products, counting errors, or other stock discrepancies.

### Purpose

The purpose of Stock Adjustment is to keep inventory records accurate and ensure that the quantity recorded in the system matches the actual quantity available in the store.

### Main Functions/Activities

- Compare recorded stock quantities with actual physical stock.
- Increase or decrease the recorded quantity of an item when necessary.
- Record the reason for a stock adjustment.
- Record damaged, lost, expired, or missing items.
- Correct errors discovered during stock counting.
- Record the date of the adjustment.
- Keep a history of stock adjustments for reference.
- Ensure that inventory records remain accurate after adjustments.
