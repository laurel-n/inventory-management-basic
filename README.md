# Inventory Management Basic
This repository is for Group 5 project - Order Fulfilment and Logistics module.
# SKU Creation and Assignment

## Short Description

SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) Creation and Assignment is the process of giving each inventory item a unique identification code. The SKU helps the store identify and distinguish products, even when they have similar names or descriptions.

## Purpose

The purpose of SKU Creation and Assignment is to provide every product with a unique identity, making it easier to organize, search, track, and manage items in the store.

## Main Functions/Activities

* Create a unique SKU for each product.
* Assign an SKU to new inventory items.
* Record product information such as name, category, brand, size, and price.
* Prevent duplicate SKU numbers.
* Search for products using their SKU.
* Update product information when necessary.
* Use SKUs to identify products during sales, stock taking, and stock movement.
  
# Stock Receiving and Recording

## Short Description

Stock Receiving and Recording is the process of receiving goods into a store and recording the items and quantities received. The products are identified using their SKU and checked against the expected delivery.

## Purpose

The purpose is to ensure that all incoming goods are correctly identified, counted, checked, and added to the store's inventory records.

## Main Functions/Activities

* Receive incoming products from suppliers.
* Identify received products using their SKU.
* Count the quantity of items received.
* Compare received goods with the purchase order or delivery note.
* Check products for damage or shortages.
* Record the quantity received.
* Update the available stock level.
* Report missing, damaged, or incorrect items.
