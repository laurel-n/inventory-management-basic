# SKU Creation and Item Identification

## Short Description

SKU (Stock Keeping Unit) Creation and Item Identification is the process of assigning a unique code to each product in a store. The SKU helps the store identify, track, and distinguish one item from another.

## Purpose

The purpose of SKU creation is to give every product a unique identity. This makes it easier to locate products, monitor stock levels, record sales, and avoid confusion between similar items.

## Main Functions/Activities

- Create a unique SKU for each product.
- Record important item details such as product name, category, size, color, and brand.
- Ensure that each SKU identifies only one specific product or product variation.
- Search for products using their SKU.
- Update item information when product details change.
- Use SKUs to support stock tracking and inventory records.
- Prevent duplicate identification of different products.
