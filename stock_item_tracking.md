# Stock Item Recording and Tracking

## Feature Name
Stock Item Recording and Tracking

## Short Description
The system records important information about store items and keeps track of their quantities and movement.

## Purpose
The purpose is to help the store know what items are available, how many are in stock, and when stock is added or removed.

## Main Functions/Activities
- Record item names and SKU numbers.
- Record item categories and descriptions.
- Record the quantity available.
- Track stock added to the store.
- Track stock removed from the store.
- Update stock quantities when changes occur.
